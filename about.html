ABOUT KICKS

Born to run. Built to last.

OUR STORY

KICKS was founded in 2024 with a single mission: to bring premium, performance-driven footwear to sneaker enthusiasts in the Philippines. From our first store in Makati to a nationwide online presence, we've stayed true to our roots — combining cutting-edge technology with iconic design. Every pair we sell is crafted for those who refuse to compromise on style or comfort.

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To inspire and empower sneaker culture in the Philippines by providing premium, performance-driven footwear that blends cutting-edge technology with iconic style. We are committed to delivering exceptional quality, unmatched comfort, and a shopping experience that keeps our customers coming back for every stride.

Our Vision

To become the leading sneaker destination in the Philippines — recognized for our curated selection, authentic products, and a community that lives and breathes kicks. We envision a world where every step you take is powered by confidence, comfort, and style.
